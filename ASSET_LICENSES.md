## Do not

- Scrape or hotlink third-party product photography
- Use stock images without a verified license
- Present SVG placeholders as real product photography in a live merchant store

## For a verified merchant

Replace SVG placeholders with licensed photography or merchant-owned assets. Prefer Cloudflare Images with AVIF/WebP variants, responsive `srcset`, accurate `sizes`, explicit dimensions and reserved aspect ratios.
